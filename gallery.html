40 screens Mac · iPhone · iPad Real data, not mockups

Each row scrolls sideways — drag or swipe to see every screen.

Home

Open the app and the month is already summed up for you.

  • Cash-flow header — money in, money out, ahead of last month or not
  • A live “left to spend” figure after bills and one-off expenses
  • Spending rings per category, with an AI insight when something drifts
  • A Sunday weekly review, 6-month trends and year-over-year comparisons
  • A dashboard you can reorder and hide sections on

Budgets

Set what each category gets — monthly and annual — and watch the rings fill.

  • Monthly limits with rings, plus annual budgets for irregular bills
  • A category list with rollover for the ones that don't reset
  • Subscriptions detected and totalled for you
  • An “overspend cover” that moves money between categories in a tap

Transactions

Every row — categorized, split, searchable, and on a calendar.

  • Add income, expenses and transfers; split one expense across categories
  • A cash-flow calendar showing every day's money at a glance
  • Scan a receipt by photo, or import a statement
  • Recurring bills on any cadence, a per-merchant view, and full-text search

Net worth & investing

Everything you own and owe, in one number with the history behind it.

  • Cash, cards, loans, property, stocks and crypto in one net-worth figure
  • Holdings with cost basis, live prices and per-position returns
  • Asset allocation against a target, and a net-worth projection
  • A cash-flow forecast of your liquid balance, and reconcile in one pass

Retirement planning

The hallmark: a plan stress-tested against history — see the full retirement page.

  • A month-by-month projection of every account to the age you choose
  • Monte Carlo across hundreds of futures, plus every real market since 1928
  • “What-if” plans — retire at 55? — compared side by side
  • A retirement cash-flow view, Social Security timing, and a PDF report

Goals & health score

Targets to aim at, and a single honest read on how you're doing.

  • Savings goals and debt-payoff goals with milestones and target dates
  • A 0–100 financial health score across five pillars, each with a next step
  • A tax estimator built on the current year's brackets

Calculators

A dozen planning calculators — the money math you'd otherwise do in a spreadsheet.

  • Mortgage, loan, refinance and rent-vs-buy
  • Debt payoff, biweekly-payoff, and a FIRE / financial-independence model
  • A paycheck breakdown and a tax estimator
  • Every one prefilled from your own numbers

Insight & reports

Where the money went, and the paperwork at the end.

  • Spending insights, trends and multi-year comparisons
  • A Report Studio that assembles an investor-grade PDF
  • Net-worth statements, income & spending, and a year-in-review
  • CSV export and per-account statements

Business

A full set of books — kept entirely separate from your personal money.

  • Invoices and estimates you build and send, with a client list
  • Job profit, work logs, crew hours and a job schedule
  • Expenses, receivables, mileage and receipts for Schedule C
  • A business dashboard, and books that never touch your personal totals

Sharing

A partner, family or friends — each seeing only what you chose.

  • A partner shares everything, both ways, with who-added-what on every row
  • Family sees a read-only summary you control
  • Friends see one scorecard and nothing else
  • All of it through your own iCloud — nothing passes through us

Fin, on your device

Ask about your own money — and watch it answer.

  • Answers from your live data: “will I make it to payday?”, “am I balanced?”
  • Builds native charts on request — “chart my spending by category”
  • Finds and totals your subscriptions, and flags the ones that went up
  • Runs on Apple's on-device model, so the question never leaves your device

Make it yours

Accent colors, widgets, alerts and bringing your history in.

  • An accent color that recolors the whole app, and configurable widgets
  • Smart alerts — large purchases, bill changes, low-balance warnings
  • Import from CSV, OFX, QFX, Excel, or a photo of a statement
  • A feature-request box that goes straight to a real person

On your wrist

The Apple Watch app is a real companion, not a notification relay.

  • This month's cash flow, budgets and net worth at a glance
  • Log an expense from your wrist in two taps
  • Goals and year-to-date progress, complications on your face

These are the real app, not mockups. Every shot is captured from a running build with the sample data set, which is also what you get if you turn on Demo Mode — so you can open the app and find these exact screens before you type in anything of your own.
