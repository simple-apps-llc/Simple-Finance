What it does

Budgets that actually close

Categories, monthly limits and rings that fill as you spend. Annual budgets and rollover for the bills that do not arrive every month.

Net worth, all of it

Cash, cards, loans, property, stocks and crypto in one number, with the history behind it. Reconcile against your bank in one pass.

Bills that tell you first

Every cadence including biweekly, reminders before they land, and a cash-flow forecast that shows the lowest point your balance will hit.

A retirement plan you can stress-test

Monte Carlo and real market history against your own numbers, with an investor-grade PDF at the end.

Share exactly as much as you mean to

A partner sees everything both ways. Family sees a summary you choose. Friends see one scorecard. All through your own iCloud.

Fin, on device

Ask about your own money in plain language. It runs on Apple's on-device model, so the questions never leave your phone.

Business money kept separate

Invoices, clients, estimates and mileage in their own books, never mixed into your personal totals.

Bring your history with you

CSV, OFX, QFX, Excel, or a photo of a statement read by OCR. Columns are matched by content, not headings. Nothing saves until you approve it.

Every day

Log it, find it, and see where the month is going.

It never asks for your bank password

Most budgeting apps hold your bank login and route every transaction through their servers. Simple Finance has no servers of its own to route them to.

Your money lives on your device — and, with sync on, in your own private iCloud, right beside your photos and messages. We can’t read it, and support can’t look up your account, because there is nothing on our side to look up.

Built independently, by a small team that uses it every day — not a company whose business is your data. Write in, and a real person answers.
