Why it exists

Most money apps ask for your bank login and keep every transaction on their servers. Simple Finance has no servers to keep them on. Your money lives on your devices — and in your own private iCloud, right beside your photos. We can’t see it. Nobody can sell it.

Where your money lives

It never asks for your bank password.

Most budgeting apps hold your bank login and route every transaction through their servers. Simple Finance has no servers of its own to route them to. Support can’t look up your account, because there is nothing on our side to look up.

  • No account to createOpen it and start. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No bank login, everWe never ask for, or hold, your bank password.
  • Your devices and your own iCloudSync is Apple’s, end to end. Off is fine too.
  • No ads, no analytics, nothing soldThere is no tracker in the app to sell anything from.
  • Works fully offlineEvery figure is worked out on the device in front of you.
