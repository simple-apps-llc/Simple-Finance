Simple Finance 3.0

A ground-up redesign of a private, all-in-one money app: budget, net worth, investments, retirement and household sharing. No bank logins, no ads. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Try it on TestFlight Simple Finance on the App Store

The TestFlight build opens in the current look. Turn on Demo Mode in Settings to see 3.0 with sample data.

On iPhone

Every screen redrawn: a calm sky that changes with each section, glass cards, and numbers that move when they change. Real screens from the 3.0 build, running on sample data.

On Mac

The same design on the Mac, built for a big window, with detail screens that open in windows of their own.

Built with Apple technologies

Live ActivitiesA budget heads-up on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island when a category passes 90%.
Apple IntelligenceFin, the built-in assistant, runs on-device.
MetalThe FIRE flame is drawn by a shader on the GPU.
CloudKit sharingHouseholds and family summaries sync through each person's own iCloud.
App Intents and ShortcutsLog an expense with Siri, or automatically after an Apple Pay purchase.
AccessibilityDynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion throughout, and fully localized in Spanish.
