Simple Finance 3.0

A ground-up redesign of a private, all-in-one money app: budgets, spending, net worth, investments, retirement, FIRE and household sharing. No bank logins, no ads. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Screen recordings from the 3.0 build on sample data. Every number on screen comes from the app's real calculations.

Everyday money

The screens you open every day, redrawn on a calm sky that shifts with each section.

Insights and reviews

The app notices things so you don't have to dig: what moved, what's trending over, and how this month compares.

Net worth and investments

Everything you own and owe on one screen, with live prices for holdings and a look ahead.

Retirement and FIRE

A full retirement planner: the chance your plan lasts, every year as a flow of money, 500 simulated markets, and history’s worst years replayed against your plan.

Goals

Saving toward something you can picture.

Together

Sharing rebuilt around how people really run money. Couples share one household. Family see a summary, both ways. Friends compare only the numbers they choose. Anything can be kept private.

Fin, tools and more

An assistant that runs on your phone, the calculators people actually use, and a business mode for side income.

On Mac

The same design built for a big window: a sidebar for every section, wide dashboards, and detail screens that open in windows of their own.

Built with Apple technologies

Live ActivitiesA budget heads-up on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island when a category passes 90%.
Apple IntelligenceFin, the built-in assistant, runs on-device.
MetalThe FIRE flame is drawn by a shader on the GPU.
CloudKit sharingHouseholds and family summaries sync through each person’s own iCloud.
App Intents and ShortcutsLog an expense with Siri, or automatically after an Apple Pay purchase.
AccessibilityDynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion throughout, and fully localized in Spanish.
