Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and in your personal iCloud if you turn on sync. We don't have servers that hold your data — see our Privacy Policy.

How do I sync across devices?

Sign into the same Apple ID and make sure iCloud is enabled for Simple Finance in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

How do I back up or move my data?

With iCloud sync on, your data is part of your iCloud. You can also export from within the app where that option is offered.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual entries in the app, or remove everything by deleting the app. iCloud copies can be removed from your iCloud settings.
