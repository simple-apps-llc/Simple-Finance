Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Simple Finance ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. We built Simple Finance to be private by design: your data stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud.

Information we access

  • Financial information you enter yourself — transactions, budgets, accounts, categories, and notes. The app does not connect to your bank and does not import data from financial institutions.
  • Notifications, only if you enable them.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud, it is synced through your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit so it's available across your devices. We do not operate our own servers that store your personal data, and we cannot see your data.

We do not sell or share your data

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs to profile you.

Third-party services

  • Apple iCloud — if you enable sync, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Your control

You can view, edit, and delete your data at any time inside the app. Deleting the app removes its on-device data; data you synced to iCloud can be removed from your iCloud settings. Health permissions can be changed any time in the Apple Health app.

Children's privacy

Simple Finance is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will appear on this page with a new "updated" date.
